A Halloween quiz for the whole office

Victoria’s Night of Frights

A free Halloween trivia game for office parties and team events, made for a big screen. Teams pick spooky questions on a game-show board, think for 30 seconds and answer. A narrator reads every question aloud.

How it plays

  • Two rounds of five Halloween and Canadian categories, worth 100–500 and then 200–1,000 points.
  • Black Cat in the Bag: the team that finds the cat must hand the question to another team.
  • Deal with the Witch: two magic wands pick a random price and the team that must answer.
  • Midnight: one final question with secret wagers, then a podium for the winning team.

Works for 2–4 teams in the room and over a video call. No sign-up, no download.
